## 🏢 Afroza Editor

I'm currently **CTO at Afroza Editor**, a technology startup based in Cameroon, where I contribute to the technical direction and development of several products designed around African use cases.

### 🛒 Afroza Marketplace

A marketplace connecting independent sellers and customers, starting in Cameroon and with a broader African vision.

**Stack:** `Laravel` · `React` · `Inertia.js` · `PostgreSQL` · `MinIO` · `Docker` · `Nginx` · `Reverb`

Seller onboarding · product management · delivery zones · reviews · real-time communication · authentication · role-based access control · payment integrations · object storage · real-time events

🌐 [afrozamarketplace.app](https://afrozamarketplace.app)

### 💳 Afroza Payment System

A fintech concept exploring digital identity, financial inclusion and digital payments — combining mobile payments, blockchain technology, stablecoin concepts and identity verification.

Developed in the context of the **Africa Digital ID Hackathon**.

## 🧪 Current Projects

### 🎓 TutorHub

An EdTech platform connecting students with tutors: tutor discovery, profiles, reservations, skills, real-time notifications and session management.

**Stack:** `Spring Boot` · `Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `PostgreSQL` · `WebSockets`

### 🚕 VORA

A mobility-oriented MVP built during the **NuxCine 2026 Hackathon** — real-time tracking, driver/courier workflows, SOS functionality, collaborative reporting and location-based services.

**Backend focus:** `Spring Boot` · `REST API` · `Real-time systems`

### 🎮 La Course de la Foi

A game project built around an African-inspired visual and cultural identity, combining game design, interactive storytelling, a mobile-first experience and modern UI/UX.

**Target release:** Christmas 2026
